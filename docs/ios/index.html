Install Prismix on iPhone or iPad

Free, no developer account, no payment. About 15 minutes the first time, then it looks after itself.

Why is this not just the App Store?

Apple charges developers a yearly fee to publish, and Prismix is free. So you install it with AltStore, a free helper app that signs Prismix with your own free Apple ID. It is a normal Apple-supported way of installing an app you made or trust.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or newer (Settings → General → About shows it).
  • A Mac or Windows computer, only for the first setup.
  • A USB cable for the phone.
  • Your Apple ID (the one you use for the App Store). Your password goes to Apple only. If you prefer, make a second free Apple ID just for this.
  • The phone and the computer on the same Wi-Fi.

Part 1. Put AltStore on your phone (once)

Mac or Windows choose your computer:

On a Mac
  1. On the Mac, open altstore.io and download AltServer for macOS.
  2. Drag AltServer into your Applications folder and open it. A small diamond icon appears at the top of the screen.
  3. Plug the phone into the Mac with the cable. Unlock the phone and tap Trust if it asks.
  4. Open Finder, click your phone in the left list, and tick Show this iPhone when on Wi-Fi. Click Apply.
  5. Click the diamond icon at the top of the screen → Install AltStore → pick your phone.
  6. Type your Apple ID and password. Wait for the message that AltStore is installed.
On Windows
  1. Install iTunes and iCloud from Apple's own website (apple.com). Not from the Microsoft Store, those versions do not work.
  2. On altstore.io download AltServer for Windows, unzip it and run setup.exe.
  3. Open AltServer from the Start menu (right-click → Run as administrator). A diamond icon appears near the clock.
  4. Plug the phone in with the cable. Unlock it and tap Trust.
  5. Open iTunes, click the small phone icon, and tick Sync with this iPhone over Wi-Fi. Click Apply.
  6. Click the diamond icon near the clock → Install AltStore → pick your phone.
  7. Type your Apple ID and password. Wait for the message that AltStore is installed.

Part 2. Tell the phone to trust it

  1. On the phone open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management (on some versions: Profiles & Device Management).
  2. Tap your Apple ID under Developer App, then Trust.
  3. Open Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, tap Developer Mode and turn it on. The phone restarts. After the restart, confirm Turn On and enter your passcode.

Developer Mode is Apple's safety switch for apps that do not come from the App Store. It is needed once.

Part 3. Install Prismix

  1. On the phone open this link. It opens AltStore and offers the Prismix source. Tap Add.
    If nothing opens: in AltStore tap Browse → Sources → + and paste
    https://github.com/rsx-xxx/prismix/releases/latest/download/altstore.json
  2. Tap Prismix in the list, then Free. Wait until it says Open.
  3. Open Prismix.

Part 4. Connect it to your computer

  1. Start Prismix on your computer. Keep the computer and phone on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. On the phone, Prismix lists your computer. Tap it. The first time, iOS asks to allow finding devices on your local network: tap Allow.
  3. No list? Tap Scan the QR code and point the camera at the code in Prismix on the computer (Band button).

Done. On an iPad the song list stays on the left while you play.

Keeping it working

  • Apple lets a free Apple ID run the app for 7 days. AltStore renews it by itself whenever the phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the computer and AltServer is running (the diamond icon is there).
  • Forgot? Open AltStore → My Apps → Refresh All while the computer is on. If the app already stopped opening, do the same, it comes back with its data.
  • A free Apple ID allows 3 apps at a time, and AltStore itself uses one slot.

Something went wrong

"Unable to Verify App" or the app will not openDo Part 2 again (Trust, and Developer Mode).
AltStore says it cannot find AltServerThe computer must be on, AltServer open (diamond icon visible) and on the same Wi-Fi as the phone. Turn off any VPN on the phone and the computer.
The phone does not appear in the Install AltStore menuUnlock the phone, replug the cable and tap Trust. On Windows check that iTunes and iCloud came from apple.com, not the Microsoft Store.
Prismix does not find my computerBoth on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network). On the phone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → turn on Prismix. Or use the QR code.
I have no computer at allLook at SideStore: after one computer setup it refreshes on the phone alone. It is more fragile, some iOS releases (26.4 for one) broke its refresh for a while, so AltStore is what this guide recommends.
